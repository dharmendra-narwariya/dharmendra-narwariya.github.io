How I work

Habits that keep projects on track

Prove it with a POC

I test the proposed approach on a small slice first, so the team and client agree on the design before the full build.

Reconcile every layer

Record counts are checked from source to serving layer, so a mismatch surfaces early and has a clear owner.

Document for the next person

Solution designs, runbooks, data mapping and lineage are part of the delivery, so the platform outlives the project.

Teach as I build

Knowledge-transfer sessions and mentoring are built into the work, so your engineers can own what I deliver.
